## Figure S10

File: Figure_S10_Nested_LASSO_and_full_pipeline_permutation (PDF/SVG/TIFF/PNG).

Purpose: distinguish internal repeat-level performance from the complete-pipeline permutation test. Panel a shows all 20 dependent repeat AUCs for four configurations with a black mean marker. Panels b/c show all 999 null AUC values and the matching observed first-repeat value. Each contrast uses 38 SCI and 10 controls. Repeats are split variability, not independent biological replication. Full-pipeline P and Holm2 values are displayed.

Observation: raw-count mean AUC is 0.979 for SCI–HC and 0.864 for SCI–TC; source sensitivity and split ranges are shown without selecting a favorable repeat. The Holm2-adjusted permutation P values are 0.002 and 0.002, respectively, under the stated sex-conditional exchangeability null.

Interpretation: retain internally evaluated association as supplementary computational evidence. Do not infer clinical independence, validate the original nine genes or claim a prospective diagnostic test. All source observations and all null draws are retained.

## Figure S11

File: Figure_S11_Clinical_BTM_resampling (PDF/SVG/TIFF/PNG).

Purpose: assess the same four frozen clinical BTM coefficients under alternative null resampling. Panel a reports the original OLS estimates and pointwise HC3 95% intervals. Panel b reports all residual-permutation and wild-bootstrap raw P values; both separate Holm4 families are entirely 1. Independent n=43 (36 SCI/7 TC); each method has 9,999 draws.

Interpretation: the added calculations do not strengthen the adjusted clinical claim. They do not establish equivalence, and they do not repair historical supervised-axis P values. Raw versus processed classification results and clinical fixed-score effects have different estimands and cannot be combined as independent confirmations.
