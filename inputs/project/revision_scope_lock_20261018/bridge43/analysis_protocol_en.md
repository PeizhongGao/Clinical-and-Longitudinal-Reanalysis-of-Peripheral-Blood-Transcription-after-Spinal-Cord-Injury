# Locked revision-stage protocol: same-43-participant gene and GO bridge, v1

Date context: 18 October 2026, Asia/Hong_Kong. This protocol was prepared after the original and previous covariate-adjusted results were known. It is an exploratory revision-stage plan, not prospective prespecification or preregistration. At sealing, no new bridge coefficients, P values, or adjusted 43-person CAMERA results have been computed. Existing M2 results are known and identified as a reuse candidate, not new evidence.

The independent unit is the participant: exactly the existing 36 SCI and 7 trauma-control participants, one sample each. Freeze their stored membership and ordering, raw clinical fields, the original supplied ComBat-normalized expression scale, the 17,498-gene tested universe, and all 4,032 existing GO BP memberships (15–500 genes). Retain the first duplicate symbol as in the existing processing code. Apply no new normalization, ComBat, log transformation, gene filtering, symbol remapping, pathway updating or outcome-based exclusion. Expression/clinical linkage snapshots are LOCAL RESTRICTED and are not authorized for publication.

Use treatment contrasts with TC, F and No as the reference levels. Concurrent TBI and prior CNS pathology retain No/Yes/Unknown categories; Unknown is a source-coded category, not an imputed absence. Center age and ISS within this exact 43-person set. Do not impute numeric data. With an intercept and no interactions, the different historical full48 centering constants affect the intercept only; they do not redefine the group coefficient or the frozen scores. Do not overwrite earlier analyses.

Fit only M0: expression ~ group; M1: expression ~ group + age_c + sex; M2: expression ~ group + age_c + sex + iss_c + tbi + prior_cns. Expected ranks are 2, 4 and 9, with residual df 41, 39 and 34, respectively. The coefficient is always groupSCI (SCI minus TC). No 41-person gene grid, additional clinical/composition covariates, feature selection, prediction, LASSO, label permutation or threshold search is included.

Use limma 3.68.4 lmFit(method='ls') followed by eBayes with proportion=0.01, stdev.coef.lim=c(0.1,4), trend=FALSE, span=NULL, robust=FALSE, winsor.tail.p=c(0.05,0.1) and legacy=NULL, with no weights. Report two-sided moderated t tests and pointwise moderated 95% CIs from topTable(confint=0.95, sort.by='none', number=Inf). These are gene-level moderated intervals, not the HC3 intervals used in existing score regressions. Retain all 17,498 effects, intervals, P values and BH values, plus model/sample/design details. DEG counts, including any clearly labeled historical absolute logFC>1 counts, are descriptive only.

For each model, run CAMERA using the same 17,498-by-43 expression matrix, locked GO indices and corresponding design, explicitly selecting groupSCI. Fix inter.gene.cor=0.01, use.ranks=FALSE, allow.neg.cor=FALSE, trend.var=FALSE, directional=TRUE, weights=NULL and sort=FALSE. Keep all 4,032 results and original term names. If an immune-term display is needed, reuse the existing eight displayed IDs regardless of significance. Do not invent a pathway effect interval. The previous unadjusted 48-person CAMERA output is only a historical anchor.

Apply BH separately to the 17,498 gene tests and the 4,032 GO tests within each model: six explicitly distinct families. Failures remain NA with the planned family size retained. Existing module-level 16/18/88/240 and within-specification families remain unchanged. Do not choose a model or family according to its smallest q, claim a jointly controlled any-model discovery, or describe pointwise CIs as simultaneous intervals. These exploratory families do not control study-wide or post-selection error rates.

The existing M2 gene table may be reused after source, membership, coding, matrix, universe, settings and numerical equivalence are verified. At execution, a single M2 reproduction check may compare logFC, CI.L, CI.R, t, P.Value and adj.P.Val by gene using absolute and relative tolerances of 1e-8. This QA reproduction is not an additional independent model. Do not select favorable values from discrepant versions; investigate first. No numerical reproduction is claimed at the current scope/input-lock stage.

Preflight checks membership, bijective participant/sample/GSM mapping, covariate equality across existing inputs, matrix finiteness, gene/GO identity, design rank, residual df and package settings only. No outcome model is fitted during preflight. Record source/snapshot SHA-256 values and the installed R/package session. At analysis execution, preserve numerical/test warnings and design diagnostics without changing samples or methods according to significance.

Interpret model-dependent coefficient, interval and DEG-count changes descriptively. ISS adjustment can alter the comparison target, and limited clinical common support remains. Changes are not a causal confounding decomposition or percentage explained; significance in one model but not another is not a tested difference between effects. Non-significant findings are retained and do not establish equivalence or absence. This bridge cannot establish SCI specificity, cell-intrinsic regulation, functional suppression or independent-cohort validation.

Stop affected work on any source/snapshot hash mismatch, membership/universe/GO discrepancy, unestimable design, unreproduced M2 result or unresolved numerical failure. A null result is not a failure. A change requires a separate dated amendment with its reason, old/new hashes and whether new effects had already been seen. Do not silently amend the sealed protocol or expand the model grid.

Future outputs comprise complete M0/M1/M2 gene and CAMERA tables, model/family summaries, numerical verification, descriptive model-change tables and software/session logs. None of those new effect outputs is represented as completed by this protocol/input package.
