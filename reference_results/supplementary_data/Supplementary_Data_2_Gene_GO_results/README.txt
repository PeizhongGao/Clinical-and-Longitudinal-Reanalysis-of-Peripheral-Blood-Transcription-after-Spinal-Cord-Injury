The public ODC-SCI 405 dataset (DOI 10.34945/F5QC7J) is CC BY 4.0. Its public clinical source fields and derived crosswalk are included with attribution in the repository at inputs/project/revision_integrated_20261012/tables/ODC405_sample_crosswalk.csv. These supplementary ZIPs focus on their named tables and are not a duplicate of all source inputs. Source-specific rights remain applicable; original-code licensing is awaiting author decision.

Genes_M0/M1/M2.csv, GO_CAMERA_M0/M1/M2.csv and bridge43_model_summary.csv are the fixed 43-participant comparison. Historical_* files retain their original participant sets, formulas and inferential roles; they are not substitutes for the fixed43 results. Historical ODC formulas and sample sizes are in Historical_ODC405_DE_model_summary.csv. The eight fixed immune terms are a subset of all 4,032 GO tests; their nonsignificance does not mean that all GO results were nonsignificant.
